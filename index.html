Trail Alliance

Wayfinding and exhibition graphics for 140 miles of shared-use trail — a sign family legible at 40 km/h, weatherproof, and installable by volunteers with a cordless drill.

WayfindingEnvironmentalIconography2024
Sign family — E-3

The problem

Four jurisdictions, four sign standards, and a trail that crosses between them eleven times. Riders were turning around at the seams.

What I made

A single sign family with a shared header band, a 14-icon set drawn on one grid, and a junction-code scheme that doubles as the 911 locator.

Specs

3 mm aluminum, reflective vinyl, three sizes off one template. Volunteer install guide fits on two sides of a letter sheet.

Outcome

Phase one covers 62 of 140 miles. Two of the four jurisdictions have adopted the icon set for trails outside the corridor.
